Particularly revealing are the excerpts from some of the least-known writings and speeches of Lenin and Stalin, which furnish valuable new insights into these two dominant figures in Soviet history. A final chapter deals with the inner-Party power struggles after Stalin's death in 1953, culminating in Khrushchev's victory.
[PARAGRAF] Comprehensive in scope and definitive in treatment, A Concise History of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union fills a long-standing need for an accurate, scholarly, and readable history of one of the world's most potent political forces.
[PARAGRAF] THE AUTHOR: John S. Reshetar, Jr., is Associate Professor of Political Science at the University of Washington, where he teaches courses in Soviet government and foreign policy. He has been a Research Fellow of the Foreign Policy Research Institute of the University of Pennsylvania and has also taught at Princeton and Yale Universities. His previously published works include The Ukrainian Revolution, 1917–1920 and Problems of Analyzing and Predicting Soviet Behavior.
