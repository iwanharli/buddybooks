[JUDUL] A CONCISE HISTORY OF THE COMMUNIST PARTY OF THE SOVIET UNION
[PARAGRAF] by John S. Reshetar, Jr.
[PARAGRAF] The history of the Communist Party of the Soviet Union has been one long struggle for power—a struggle marked by duplicity, opportunism, and ruthlessness. Despite the vast range of literature published on this subject, no single book has yet dealt with it compactly, succinctly, and at the same time in a fully detailed fashion. John Reshetar has now written such a history—an objective and thoroughly documented account of this internecine struggle, covering the early formative days of the Party, its rise to power through revolution, its subsequent rule up to the present, and the policies and actions of the men who molded the Party and led it.
[PARAGRAF] This work is based almost exclusively on primary source materials, including speeches of prominent Party spokesmen, stenographic reports of Party Congresses, Central Committee and Party Conference resolutions, and important theoretical writings. As a result, the author has been able to filter out of his presentation the usual myths embodied and transmitted in other accounts.
[PARAGRAF] Beginning with the philosophical foundations of Russian Communism, Dr. Reshetar carefully examines the precursors of today's CPSU, tracing the Party's origins and development prior to 1918, the evolution of Bolshevism under Lenin's determined leadership, and the conflicts with the Mensheviks and other factions. The emphasis throughout is on ideological developments and shifts in the Party line, organizational and personnel changes, purges, and the elements of contradiction as well as of continuity and change in the Party's history.
