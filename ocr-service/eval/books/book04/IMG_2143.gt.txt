[JUDUL] PRESIDEN REPUBLIK INDONESIA
[PARAGRAF] Swami Vivekananda! What a name! He was one of the men, who gave so much inspiration to me, — inspiration to be strong, inspiration to be a servant of God, inspiration to be a servant of my country, inspiration to be a servant of the poor, inspiration to be a servant of mankind. He was it, who said: „We have wept long enough; no more weeping, but stand on your feet, and be men!"
[PARAGRAF] Soekarno.—
[PARAGRAF] 4/10 1963
